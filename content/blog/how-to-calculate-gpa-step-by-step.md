Calculating your GPA doesn't have to be confusing. Whether you're a high school student juggling semesters or a university student tracking cumulative performance, understanding the exact steps and formulas gives you control over your academic goals. This guide walks through the practical steps required to compute both semester and cumulative GPA, explains how credit hours affect the final calculation, and clarifies how weighted courses (AP, Honors, IB) change grade point values. We'll also show how to convert percentage scores to commonly used GPA scales (4.0, 4.3, and 10.0) and provide a sample calculation you can follow. Later sections include tips for raising your GPA, common institutional policies for repeated courses, and recommendations for scholarship-focused grade planning.
